📌 Project Description: Sales Data Analysis & Visualization
This project is designed to analyze and visualize sales data using Power BI and Python. The goal is to provide valuable insights into sales performance, customer behavior, and product profitability through interactive dashboards and in-depth analytics.

By leveraging historical sales transactions, customer demographics, and product details, the project helps businesses make data-driven decisions and optimize their strategies. The analysis covers key aspects such as revenue trends, customer segmentation, geographical sales distribution, and product performance.

🎯 Key Objectives
✔ Monitor sales trends over different time periods.
✔ Compare actual sales vs. budgeted targets to evaluate performance.
✔ Segment customers based on purchasing behavior.
✔ Identify best-selling products and those with the highest profitability.
✔ Visualize geographical sales distribution to detect growth opportunities.

🛠 Technologies Used
✅ Power BI – For interactive dashboards and real-time data visualization.
✅ Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn) – For data preprocessing and additional analytics.
✅ DAX & Power Query – For advanced calculations and data transformations.
✅ Excel & CSV – For raw data extraction and processing.

🚀 Expected Outcomes
📊 Interactive Power BI dashboards that provide clear insights into sales and customer trends.
📌 Data-driven decision-making by identifying key sales patterns and revenue drivers.
📍 Actionable analytics to improve marketing strategies and optimize product offerings.

This project serves as a powerful business intelligence tool for organizations looking to enhance their sales strategies and maximize revenue.
